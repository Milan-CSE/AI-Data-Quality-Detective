## 4. Key Architectural Insights & Trade-Offs
1. **Multivariate Blind Spot of IQR**: IQR evaluates features one-by-one. When an anomaly consists of individually plausible values that violate natural joint correlations (e.g. 22-year-old with 26 years of experience), IQR has **zero recall** on those records. Isolation Forest captures these joint anomalies in tree-partitioned feature space.
2. **Separation of Concerns**: Isolation Forest is an unsupervised numerical model. Asking it to detect typos in strings or duplicate rows leads to poor precision. Deterministic validation rules achieve 100% precision on explicit domain contracts, while ML handles complex distributional and relational anomalies.
